Standing on good shoulders

Ukuri, Kinyarwanda for “truth”, is a thin layer over open data and open-source work. These are the people and projects that make it possible.

data & apis

  • OpenAlex

    Open catalogue of ~250M scholarly works. Powers search, DOI lookup, citation counts, retraction flags and every reference audit.

    CC0
  • Kroki

    Renders the citation maps on the Cite and Reading list pages from Mermaid diagram source.

    MIT

libraries

  • PDF.js

    Mozilla’s PDF renderer. Reads the References section of uploaded papers entirely in your browser.

    Apache-2.0
  • Express

    The Node.js server behind the /api proxy, caching and static files.

    MIT
  • dotenv

    Loads server configuration from environment files.

    BSD-2-Clause
  • Font Awesome

    Interface icons in the sidebar, search box and map controls, loaded from cdnjs.

    CC BY 4.0

typefaces

  • Fraunces

    Headings, paper titles and APA references. By Undercase Type.

    OFL-1.1
  • Inter

    Interface text. By Rasmus Andersson.

    OFL-1.1
  • JetBrains Mono

    Labels, counts and metadata. By JetBrains.

    OFL-1.1

design

  • Imigongo

    The clay accent and the zigzag and diamond motifs are drawn from Imigongo, the geometric cow-dung panel art of Rwanda’s Nyakarambi region.

    tradition
  • opencode

    The flat panel, hairline and monospace layout takes cues from the opencode web interface.

    inspiration
